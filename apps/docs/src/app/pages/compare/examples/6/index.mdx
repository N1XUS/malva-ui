The layers project arbitrary markup — here two renderings of the same invoice summary, the previous layout on the start side and the redesign on the end side. The taller layer sizes the surface and the other stretches to fill it, so both cards always meet the divider edge to edge.

Projected content is display-only: the surface owns every pointer gesture so a press anywhere moves the divider, and the layers are pointer-transparent. Keep interactive controls out of the tab order (or out of the layers) — the comparison is the control here, not the buttons inside it.
