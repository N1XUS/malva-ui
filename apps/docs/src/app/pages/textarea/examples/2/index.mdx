---
title: Auto-Resize
---

When `autoResize` is enabled, the textarea grows with its content. Use `minRows` and `maxRows` to clamp the height range. The resize handle is hidden in auto-resize mode.

The height follows the value however it is written. Typing is the obvious case; **Load sample text** and **Clear** set the bound signal directly, without touching the element, and the field resizes just the same.
