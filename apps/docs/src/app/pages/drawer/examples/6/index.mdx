---
title: Playground
---

One drawer driven by controls outside it: pick any of the four `position`s, a fixed `size` or `resizable` with a `snapPoints` preset (free resize, thirds, or the bottom-sheet ladder), and toggle the `hasBackdrop` dim. Inside, `mlv-drawer-sections` in the header navigates the body's `mlvDrawerSection`s, and the Markup section prints the exact `<mlv-drawer>` tag for the current settings. Note that with `resizable` the opening size is `defaultSnap` (a viewport percentage), not `size`.
