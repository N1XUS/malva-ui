---
title: Bottom Sheet
---

A bottom drawer with `resizable` and `snapPoints` inputs becomes a **bottom sheet**. The drag handle snaps to 40%, 80%, or 100% of the viewport after release. Swipe down fast to dismiss. Keyboard: with focus on the handle, **Arrow Up/Down** move it the way they point (up grows the sheet), **Home/End** jump to extremes, and **Escape** closes like anywhere else in the drawer.
