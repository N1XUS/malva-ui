---
title: Variants
---

Buttons come in nine visual variants selected with the `variant` input:
`primary`, `secondary`, `outlined`, `accent`, `transparent`, `elevated`,
`error`, `warning`, and `info`. Each variant maps to a distinct background,
text, and hover treatment for different levels of emphasis.

At most two saturated fills render together in one view — `primary` and
`accent` (AB-R8). `error`, `warning`, and `info` are shown in a separate row
below: they are reserved for a single blocking call to action — a confirm or
discard button in a dialog, for example — and never appear in a demo row
alongside `primary`.
