---
title: Icon-only buttons
---

`shape="square"` and `shape="circle"` are the icon-only shapes, and a button using one of
them needs no further annotation:

- **`mlvButtonIcon` is optional.** A lone projected `<svg>` is detected after render, so the
  button gets the `mlv-button--icon-only` treatment and the icon is sized to the button
  instead of keeping its intrinsic `24px`. Keep the directive when the button's label
  content is toggled at runtime — the inference does not re-observe projected text.
- **The default variant is `secondary`, not `primary`.** A row of un-annotated icon actions
  would otherwise render as a wall of saturated calls-to-action. An explicit `variant`, or
  one inherited from `mlv-button-group` / `mlv-button-split` / `mlv-button-toggle`, still
  wins.
