## Custom templates

Three structural directives override parts of the default rendering while the
chat keeps ownership of grouping, alignment, the meta row, and animations.

`[mlvChatMessageDef]` with `mlvChatMessageDefType` replaces the bubble body for
messages carrying a matching `type`, receiving the message plus `isOwn`,
`groupPosition`, and `quote` as context — the `quote` flag lets one template
serve both the full bubble and its condensed form inside a reply.

`[mlvChatAuthorDef]` replaces the plain author name above other-authored groups
(here an avatar and a role badge), and `[mlvChatDateDef]` replaces the
Today/Yesterday pill. Typeless messages keep the built-in renderers, so custom
templates are strictly additive.
