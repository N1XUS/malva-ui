## Media and audio

Attachments are declared on the message itself. `image` and `gif` entries and
`video` posters share one adaptive grid: a single item renders large with its
`width`/`height` reserving the aspect ratio before load, two to four form a
2-column grid, and anything beyond four collapses behind a `+N` overlay. Every
cell is a button that emits `(mediaClick)` with both the message and the
attachment — the chat deliberately ships no lightbox, so the host decides how to
open media.

`video` shows a play badge and duration chip rather than inline player chrome;
`gif` autoplays muted on a loop. `audio` attachments render their own player
with a seek slider, and only one audio message plays at a time within a single
`mlv-chat`. An attachment carrying `uploadProgress` dims and shows a progress
overlay until the field is cleared.
