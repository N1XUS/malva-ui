---
title: Vertical drum
---

The default `orientation` is `vertical` — the classic drum roll. Scroll, click a
row, or focus the strip and use ArrowUp / ArrowDown, Home / End or type-ahead;
whatever lands on the centre stripe is the selected value.

The strip renders `String(value)` on its own. The zero-padded two-digit numeral
is `mlv-time-picker`'s format, passed in through `displayWith` — a year strip
wants `2078`, not `78`.
