---
title: Programmatic control & two-way binding
---

Grab a template reference to `mlv-accordion` (`#acc`) and call `expandAll()` /
`collapseAll()` imperatively — both require `[multiExpandable]="true"`.

Each `mlv-accordion-item` also exposes a two-way `[(expanded)]` model, so you
can drive and observe an individual section's state from your component (here
the badge mirrors the Billing panel).
