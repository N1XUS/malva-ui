---
title: 12-hour AM/PM mode
---

Set `mode="12h"` to display a 12-hour clock with an **AM/PM** toggle column. Hours cycle from 1 to 12. The component always stores and emits values in **24-hour format** (`HH:mm`), so `09:00` means 9 AM and `17:30` means 5:30 PM — no conversion needed on the consumer side.

Click the AM/PM buttons or navigate the hour column past 12 to switch the period.
