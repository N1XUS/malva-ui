## Nested Submenu

Nested submenus use `[isSubmenuTrigger]="true"` on a `mlv-menu-item` to open a secondary `mlv-menu` to the right via `MlvPopupService`. Triangle pointer tracking keeps the submenu open while the cursor moves diagonally from the parent item toward the panel. Selecting an item from the submenu closes the whole menu stack, including the root menu.
