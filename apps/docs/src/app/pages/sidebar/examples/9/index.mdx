## Responsive (collapseBelow)

`collapseBelow="md"` makes the sidebar switch itself to an offcanvas drawer below
768px — no `matchMedia` wiring and no `mode` juggling in the host. Resize the
window across the breakpoint to see it flip. The `mlv-sidebar-trigger` sits
outside `<mlv-sidebar>` (bound with `[sidebar]="nav"`) because a closed drawer
renders none of its projected content.
