---
title: Sizes & Shapes
---

The avatar supports six sizes: `xs` (24px), `s` (32px), `m` (40px, default), `l` (56px), `xl` (80px), and `xxl` (96px).

Two shape variants are available: `circle` (default, fully rounded) and `square` (with an 8px border radius via `--mlv-radius-2`).

Use the `mlvColorFromText` pipe to automatically generate a deterministic background color from a name string.
