---
title: Custom action content
---

Project an `ng-template` with `mlvSpeedDialItemDef` to render your own content inside every action button — the template receives the item as `$implicit` and its `index`. The button itself (its `role="menuitem"`, accessible name from `label`, tooltip and disabled state) stays owned by the speed dial. `itemVariant` picks the `mlv-button` variant of the actions, `variant` that of the trigger.
