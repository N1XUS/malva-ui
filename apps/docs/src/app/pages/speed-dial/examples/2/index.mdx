## Radial layouts

`type="circle"` spreads the actions evenly around the trigger, starting at 12 o'clock. `semi-circle` fills the half facing `direction`, and `quarter-circle` fills the corner named by a two-part direction (`up-left`, `up-right`, `down-left`, `down-right`). `radius` is the distance in px from the trigger's centre to each action's centre.
