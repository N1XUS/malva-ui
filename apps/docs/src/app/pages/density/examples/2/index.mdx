## Per-Component Density via hostDirectives
Integrate density support into any component by adding `MlvDensityDirective` as a `hostDirective` and providing the `MLV_DENSITY_ELEMENT` injection token with your component's BEM block name. The directive applies `mlv-{element}--{density}` modifier classes to the host and exposes `effectiveDensity` for programmatic reads. Expose `mlvDensity` in `inputs` to let consumers override density per instance.
