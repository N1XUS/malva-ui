---
title: Floating Variant & All Density Levels
---

The `floating` attribute elevates the chip with a box-shadow — ideal for chips over imagery or complex backgrounds. Use `mlvDensity` to control size across all five density levels: **tight** → **compact** → **comfortable** (default) → **spacious** → **airy**. Density can be set per-chip or inherited from an ancestor with a density modifier class.
