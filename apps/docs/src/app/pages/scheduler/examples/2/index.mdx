## All-day and multi-day events

`allDay: true` events use **exclusive** ends at midnight: a two-day event ends at the start of the
third day. They render as bars in the all-day row of the week/day views and as spanning bars in the
month grid. A timed event that crosses midnight is clipped per day in the time grid, and a timed
event of 24 hours or more is promoted to a lane bar. Bars that continue beyond the visible range or a
row edge show a continuation chevron.
