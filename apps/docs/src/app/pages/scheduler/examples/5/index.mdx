---
title: Working hours, slots and hidden days
---

`minTime` / `maxTime` clip the time axis; `slotDuration` sets the row height and the keyboard step;
`snapDuration` (defaults to the slot) is the pointer snap for moves and resizes. `businessHours`
shades the working range and the current-time line (`showCurrentTime`) follows the wall clock.
`scrollToCurrentTime` opens the grid centred on the current time when that time is inside the
visible axis, and otherwise on the business-hours start. `hiddenDays` removes weekdays from the
month and week views — the day view still renders a hidden weekday it is pointed at, and only
steps over it when navigating — and `firstDayOfWeek` rotates the week (`0` = Sunday).
