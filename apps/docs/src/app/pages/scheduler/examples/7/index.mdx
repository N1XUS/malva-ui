---
title: Range selection, density and locale
---

Drag across empty cells (or press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Arrow</kbd> from a focused cell and confirm
with <kbd>Enter</kbd>) to emit `rangeSelect` with `{ start, end, allDay, source }`; double-click a
slot for `slotDoubleClick`. The scheduler is density-aware through `mlvDensity` and reads its
toolbar, hint and announcement strings from `@malva-ui/i18n` — switch the docs language and
direction in the preferences popup to see the translated labels, the mirrored toolbar and the
flipped grids. Weekday names and hour labels come from the date adapter, not from `@malva-ui/i18n`:
provide `MLV_DATE_LOCALE` (or your own `MlvDateAdapter`) to localize those.
