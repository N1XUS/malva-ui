---
title: Body layout
---

`bodyLayout` controls how `.mlv-card__body` lays out its projected children.

- `'none'` (default) leaves the body a plain block — existing cards are unchanged.
- `'stack'` makes the body a vertical flex stack separated by `--mlv-card-body-gap`, which
  replaces the wrapper `<div>` with its own `gap` that multi-child cards used to hand-roll.

The gap follows the size preset (`s` → `--mlv-spacing-2`, `m` → `--mlv-spacing-3`, `l` →
`--mlv-spacing-4`) and can be overridden per card:

```html
<mlv-card bodyLayout="stack" style="--mlv-card-body-gap: 1.5rem">…</mlv-card>
```
