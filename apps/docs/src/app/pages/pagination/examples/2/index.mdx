## Items per page with "All" option

Include `Infinity` in `perPageOptions` to offer an "All (Z)" choice that puts every item on one page. Options larger than `totalItems` are dropped, so a short list simply offers fewer choices — and the selector disappears entirely once `totalItems` fits within the smallest option. The active label expands with a width and opacity transition, which `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off.
