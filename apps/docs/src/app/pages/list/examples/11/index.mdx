## Rich Selectable Rows

`mlv-list[selectable]` and `mlv-list-item[value]` are not limited to plain
text options — the same avatar/title/byline row shape from the examples
above works as a keyboard-navigable, single-select listbox. Arrow keys move
the roving selection, Home/End jump to the ends, and clicking or pressing
Space/Enter commits it, exactly as in the plain-text single selection
example.
