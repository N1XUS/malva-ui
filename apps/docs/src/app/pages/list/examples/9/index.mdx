---
title: Action Rows With Overflow
---

Each row carries exactly one trailing action, matching common "single action"
and "multiple actions with no content on the right" cell patterns.
Most rows spell their action out as a `variant="secondary"` button; once a
row would need more than one action, it collapses into a single
`variant="transparent" shape="circle"` overflow trigger that opens an
`mlv-menu` instead of crowding the row with several buttons.
