---
title: Fieldsets
---

`fieldset[mlvFieldset]` groups controls in a grid that inherits the form gap. `columns="auto"` (default) auto-fits columns of at least `minColumnWidth`; a number caps the column count and still collapses on narrow containers. `mlvFieldsetSpan="full"` stretches a control across the row; project a native `<legend>` for rich headings.
