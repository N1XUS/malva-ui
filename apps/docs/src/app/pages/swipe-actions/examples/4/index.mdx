---
title: Tones
---

`tone` fills an action: the default `neutral` surface for a secondary "More", the brand `accent`, or one of the semantic `info` / `success` / `warning` / `danger` tones. An action is a full-height solid block that stacks its projected Lucide icon above the label; keep the label to one or two words, or drop it and give the button an `aria-label`.

Inside a side, the outermost action is the hero: while the side is only partly revealed every action pins to the row's edge and the outermost paints on top, then the inner ones slide out from beneath it as the swipe completes.
