title: Controlled
---

`opened` is a two-way model of the **settled** side: `'start'`, `'end'` or `null`. It changes once a swipe comes to rest, not while the finger is still moving, so a consumer never sees intermediate positions. Writing it scrolls the row there — smoothly, or as an instant jump under `prefers-reduced-motion`. `open(side)`, `close()` and `peek(side)` are the imperative equivalents on the component instance.

`openedChange` reports what the row decided on its own — a swipe coming to rest, an action click, Escape, a press outside — and stays silent for a value you wrote, exactly like any other `model()`. The log below shows both: the write from a button, then only the row's own reports.
