---
title: Tones & radius
---

`tone` colours the active pill with the matching **pale** background and semantic text — `neutral` (default, boxed-tabs white), `accent`, `info`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`.

Geometry and colours are CSS custom properties on the host, so a `style` override or a theme rule is enough: `--mlv-segmented-radius` (track; the pill and items stay concentric), `--mlv-segmented-track-padding`, `--mlv-segmented-track-bg`, `--mlv-segmented-pill-bg`, `--mlv-segmented-active-color`, `--mlv-segmented-separator-color`, `--mlv-segmented-indicator-duration`.
