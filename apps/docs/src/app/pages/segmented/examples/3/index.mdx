---
title: Form control
---

`mlv-segmented` is a signal-form control like `mlv-radio-group`: it works with `[(ngModel)]`, `[formField]` and `[(value)]`, and inherits `label`, `description`, `message`, `required`, `readonly` and `disabled`. Group `disabled` disables every segment; a single `<button … disabled>` is skipped by both click and arrow keys.

Under `[formField]` the framework owns `required` and `disabled` — declare them in the schema (`required(path.plan)`) instead of as attributes, which Angular rejects on a bound field.
